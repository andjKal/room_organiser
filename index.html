Room Organizer Instructions

  • Drag & Drop: Click and drag an object from the list into the floor plan.
  • Move Objects: Click and drag an object on the floor plan to reposition it.
  • Rotate Objects: Right-click on an object in the floor plan to rotate it by 15° clockwise.
  • Spacing Rules: Objects will automatically adjust to maintain spacing and avoid overlapping.
